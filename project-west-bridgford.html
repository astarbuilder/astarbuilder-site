PROJECT CASE STUDY

Modern Kitchen Extension

West Bridgford, Nottingham

Replacing an existing conservatory with a larger flat roof extension and opening the existing kitchen into the new space to create a bright, modern and more practical family living area.

01

The Existing Home

The starting point was an existing conservatory at the rear of the property. The aim was to replace it with a larger, better insulated extension that would feel like a natural part of the house.

02

Developing the Design

The initial design work established the proportions, roof form, glazing and overall relationship between the new extension and the existing property.

03

Building the 3D Concept

The design was then developed into a detailed 3D model. This allowed the exterior proportions and the new open-plan interior to be explored before moving on to realistic visualisation.

04

Bringing the Design to Life

The final stage transformed the 3D model into realistic visualisations, helping show how the extension could look and feel before construction begins.
